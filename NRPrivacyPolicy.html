NR Tracker – Privacy Policy

NR Tracker does not collect, store or share any personal information.

The app has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and does not request internet access. Game state, settings and layouts are saved only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Links in the About screen open in your web browser, and anything you do there is covered by that website's own policy.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
